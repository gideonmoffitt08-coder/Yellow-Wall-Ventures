About

A wall is built one brick at a time.

We believe great companies are not products of luck — they are assembled, deliberately, by founders with vision and operators with grit. Yellow Wall Ventures invests at pre-seed and seed, then rolls up our sleeves alongside founders to build something durable.

Headquartered in Boston with partners in New York and San Francisco, we lead and co-lead rounds from $500K to $4M, with reserves to support our companies through Series B and beyond.

  • Conviction-led investing across fintech, AI infrastructure, climate, and consumer.
  • Hands-on support: hiring, GTM, fundraising, and board craft.
  • Long-term partners — we sign on for the journey, not the round.

How we partner

More than capital.

Our partners have founded, scaled, and exited companies. We bring that lived experience to every term sheet we write.

First Checks

We lead pre-seed and seed rounds with conviction. No party rounds, no waiting for signal — we move fast when we believe.

Talent & Hiring

Access to a vetted network of operators, engineers, and GTM leaders. We help our companies make their first 25 hires.

GTM & Growth

Concrete playbooks for your first ten customers, your first dollar of revenue, and your first $1M ARR.

Follow-on Capital

We reserve 2–3x our initial check to lean in at the inflection point — when conviction matters most.

Network & Intros

Warm introductions to downstream investors, design partners, and the operators who have done it before.

Board Craft

Thoughtful, founder-aligned board members who optimize for long-term value, not quarterly theatrics.
